ROCK N' BIRA BATTLEPrototype 1

Turn your phone sideways to play

On your phone

Turn the phone sideways. Drag the left stick to walk, crouch and jump; the arrow combos below work by sliding your thumb (for example ↓ then →). The buttons on the right are A S D F; press two at once with two fingers for the two-button specials: S+D (Panama / Welcome to the Jungle), A+D (Spanish Fly / Sweet Child), S+F (David Lee Roth / Axl Rose) and A+S for the super (5150 / Appetite). On the fighter select screen, tap a portrait to pick it, then tap it again or tap FIGHT!; then tap a stage and FIGHT! to start. Tap the screen after a match to go back to fighter select.

Click the game before playing so the keyboard responds.

P1 (you)

← → walk · hold back to block · ↓ crouch (low block) · ↑ jump (with ←/→ to jump back/forward) · air kick with D or F

A light punch · S heavy punch · D light kick · F heavy kick

Eddie specials

Facing right; on the other side the arrows are mirrored. Only the 4 arrow keys, one after the other: tap them quickly and press the button while holding the last arrow.

Eruption: ↓ → + A or S. Fireball; faster with S. Whoever gets hit is toasted.

Panama: S + D together (or ↓ → + D or F). Eddie shows up in a Lamborghini and runs the opponent flat as a pancake.

Spanish Fly: A + D together. Eddie taps on a classical nylon guitar and sends hypnotic waves; whoever gets hit yawns, falls asleep on the floor and is wide open (hit him to wake him up).

Jump!: S + F together (or → ↓ + D or F). David Lee Roth drops from the sky in a split, right on top of the opponent.

Dive Bomb: ↓ ← + A or S. Three bombs fall from the sky where the opponent stands.

Shark Bite: ↓ ← + D or F. Pulls out the Ibanez "Shark" and bites 3 times.

Brown Sound: ↓ ↓ + A or S (tap down twice). A column of distortion bursts around Eddie; great against jumpers.

Bumblebee Swarm: ↓ ↓ + D or F. A swarm of bees chases the opponent; three stings and he swells up.

Poundcake Drill: → → + A or S (tap forward twice). A Makita drill on the strings, multiple hits.

5150 (super): A + S together when the Feedback bar is full (the bar under your health; it fills as you hit and get hit, and flashes when ready). Tapping, a knee slide and a lightning beam across the stage.

Slash specials

Same arrow combos as Eddie, facing right.

Top Hat: ↓ → + A or S. Throws his top hat like a boomerang; it flies far and comes back. Faster with S.

Welcome to the Jungle: S + D together (or ↓ → + D or F). Dashes in and a giant python strikes.

Sweet Child: A + D together. The famous riff sends notes and hearts; whoever gets hit can't stop dancing and is wide open.

You Could Be Mine: S + F together (or → ↓ + D or F). Slash whistles and Axl Rose knee-slides across the stage. It's a low hit: block it crouching.

November Rain: ↓ ← + A or S. Rain starts and a grand piano drops on the opponent. Can't be blocked crouching.

Double Neck: ↓ ← + D or F. Swaps to the double-neck Gibson and hits 3 times.

Paradise City: ↓ ↓ + A or S. Stage pyro bursts from the floor around him; great against jumpers.

Snake Pit: ↓ ↓ + D or F. Little snakes fall out of his top hat and slither after the opponent. Low hit: block crouching, or jump over them.

Smoke Screen: → → + A or S (tap forward twice). Takes a long drag and blows a huge smoke cloud that rolls slowly at the opponent; whoever gets caught coughs and gets dizzy.

Appetite (super): A + S together with the Feedback bar full. The stage goes dark, rain falls, Slash plays the solo and lightning strikes the opponent.

Other

Fighter select: ← → to choose, A or Enter to pick, then A or Enter again. Next, pick the stage with ← → (7 rock-history stages, or ? for a random one) and A or Enter to fight (Esc goes back). A human 2P picks with J L and 7. After a match, Enter goes back to fighter select.
