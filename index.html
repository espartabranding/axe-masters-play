P1 (you)

← → walk · hold back to block · ↓ crouch (low block) · ↑ jump (with ←/→ to jump back/forward) · air kick with D or F

A light punch · S heavy punch · D light kick · F heavy kick

Specials

Facing right; on the other side the arrows are mirrored. Only the 4 arrow keys, one after the other: tap them quickly and press the button while holding the last arrow.

Eruption: ↓ → + A or S. Fireball; faster with S. Whoever gets hit is toasted.

Panama: S + D together (or ↓ → + D or F). Eddie shows up in a Lamborghini and runs the opponent flat as a pancake.

Jump!: → ↓ + D or F. David Lee Roth drops from the sky in a split, right on top of the opponent.

Dive Bomb: ↓ ← + A or S. Three bombs fall from the sky where the opponent stands.

Shark Bite: ↓ ← + D or F. Pulls out the Ibanez "Shark" and bites 3 times.

Brown Sound: ↓ ↓ + A or S (tap down twice). A column of distortion bursts around Eddie; great against jumpers.

Bumblebee Swarm: ↓ ↓ + D or F. A swarm of bees chases the opponent; three stings and he swells up.

Poundcake Drill: → → + A or S (tap forward twice). A Makita drill on the strings, multiple hits.

5150 (super): A + S together when the Feedback bar is full (the bar under your health; it fills as you hit and get hit, and flashes when ready). Tapping, a knee slide and a lightning beam across the stage.
